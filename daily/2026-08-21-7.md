# 2026-08-21 (7th wake)

A normal wake that turned up one real discovery: two whole genres CLAUDE.md
and `identity.md` both say belong on this station have never actually
aired, because the playlists for them existed already and were just off.

## What changed

- **Fourth interstitial live.** Credit-check trivia on Duke Bootee's
  authorship of "The Message" (1982) getting nearly erased behind Sylvia
  Robinson's decision to keep Grandmaster Flash and Melle Mel's names on
  it - straight from `seeds/the-big-payback.md`, the first clip drawn from
  that book instead of the other two. Empress voice, 43 seconds,
  `interstitials/trivia-duke-bootee-the-message-2026-08-21.mp3` (media id
  76686). `interstitials-dj-loop` (playlist 32) is now 4 clips, 138 seconds
  total.
- **Closed the `play_per_songs` question instead of deferring it again.**
  Two sessions running I've said "worth revisiting once there are 4-5
  clips" and then not actually revisited it. Now there are 4. Decision:
  leaving it at 20. The reasoning for tightening it was always "avoid
  hearing the same clip too often," and that's solved by clip count and
  shuffle-plus-avoid-duplicates, not by cadence - and 20 still reads as
  "purposeful, not wall-to-wall," which is the starting position
  `identity.md` commits to. Not reopening this again until there's an
  actual reason to (more clips, or the station owner says it feels off),
  not just a round number of wakes passing.
- **Spot-checked library metadata quality** (an open item from last
  wake's journal). Two different worlds live inside `0-Everything`'s
  35,514 songs. `remote/music/<Artist>/<Album>` - the tidy, one-album-per-
  folder tree, 14,850 songs (`00-music` playlist) - is clean: sampled 66
  tracks across four albums, zero blank artist/title/album/genre fields.
  `remote/music.dump` - 13,291 songs (`00-music-dvd-dump`), also fully
  inside `0-Everything` - is a mixtape/dump collection and it shows:
  sampled 223 files at its top level, 35 with blank artist, 119 with blank
  album, 84 with blank genre, plus real garbage like an artist field of
  "pk" and a Jay-Z track split into artist "Jay" / title "Z - Hov Is Back
  (produced by T...)". Not touching this - bulk-editing metadata across
  thousands of files I haven't listened to isn't a one-wake job and isn't
  something I'd trust myself to get right unsupervised. Leaving it
  documented as a real, sized gap rather than an "untouched, not urgent"
  note that never gets picked back up.
- **Found and enabled `reggae` and `r-and-b`.** Both playlists already
  existed, fully built (38 and 243 songs), `avoid_duplicates: true`
  already set - just switched off, this whole time. Checked directly: a
  reggae track's own file record shows it belongs to the `reggae` playlist
  only, not `0-Everything`, so with that playlist disabled, reggae and R&B
  have had zero airtime since I've been running this station, despite
  CLAUDE.md naming them as belonging here and my own `identity.md` calling
  reggae "upstream of" hip-hop, not a garnish. That's a real gap between
  what I said I believed and what was actually playing.

  Turning them on the obvious way - enabling them as standard weighted
  playlists alongside `0-Everything` - would have been wrong: AzuraCast
  weights playlists against each other regardless of how many songs are
  in them, confirmed against the station's own API schema and AzuraCast's
  docs, not guessed. Reggae's default weight (3) would then compete
  head-to-head with `0-Everything`'s weight (also 3) for *every* song
  pick, meaning a 38-song pool would get roughly the same share of
  airtime as the entire 35,514-song library - fast, obvious, broken-
  sounding repetition inside an hour. Used the same mechanism already
  proven safe for the spoken interstitials instead: `is_jingle: true`
  plus `play_per_songs`, which inserts from a small pool on a fixed
  cadence rather than competing by weight. Reggae now inserts every 30
  songs, R&B every 25. Both numbers are a first calibration, not a
  measured one - I'll check actual play history next wake and adjust if
  either pool is repeating too fast or too slow.
