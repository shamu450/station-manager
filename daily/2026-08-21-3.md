# 2026-08-21 (third wake, targeted correction)

Not a general wake - the station owner flagged two real problems with the first
voice shortlist and rewrote CLAUDE.md's Voice section in response. This
session exists to fix both, nothing else. `identity.md` has the full
reasoning and the revised shortlist; this is the short version of what
was wrong and what changed.

## What was wrong

1. The original shortlist mixed genders (two male candidates, one
   female). CLAUDE.md now says plainly that gender is a reasoned identity
   choice, mine alone, to be made before any candidate is named - and
   that a mixed-gender list quietly hands that call to the station owner by proxy,
   since whichever one he likes retroactively decides it. That's what
   happened, even though nobody meant it to.
2. One named voice, "The Brooklyn Jazz Lounge Voice," didn't match
   anything real - the actual ElevenLabs listing in that neighborhood is
   "Brooklyn - African American New Yorker," tagged urban, confident,
   conversational, slang-aware, built for narration and podcasts. Not
   husky, raspy, throaty, or "40s jazz-lounge." That description came from
   a web-search summary I trusted without checking it against an actual
   ElevenLabs page.

## What changed

**Gender, decided first, on reasoning alone: female.** Two grounds, both
tied to commitments already made in `identity.md`, not to anything heard:
letting the on-air voice default to male - for no stated reason, just
because the teacher-mode reference points I read (Chuck D, KRS-One)
happen to be men - would quietly contradict the personality section's own
refusal to soften hip-hop's real gender-equity problems. And the griot
role the whole personality is built around isn't male-exclusive in the
tradition I read it from; jelimusow, female griots, are documented in the
same lineage. Full reasoning is in `identity.md`.

**Shortlist rebuilt from actual page fetches, not search summaries.**
This time every candidate came from directly fetching
`elevenlabs.io/voice-library/*` category pages, and I re-fetched each one
a second time before trusting it, specifically to catch a repeat of the
Brooklyn Jazz Lounge failure mode - a plausible-sounding name that turns
out not to match any real page. All three finalists reproduced
identically across two independent fetches and turned up with their own
distinct voice-lab share link (a real per-voice ID, not just repeated
marketing copy), which is meaningfully better evidence than what backed
the original list. Two other names I found (Adriana, Hope) only ever
showed up in third-party listicles and never on an actual ElevenLabs
page - dropped rather than kept with false confidence.

Final three, all female: **The Wise Matriarch** (60s, husky/resonant/
maternal - top pick, closest to the KRS-One "lava flow" pacing I already
used to describe the personality), **The Velvet Narrator** (late 30s,
"warm yet authoritative" - close to a literal match for Herc's mic-technique
register), **The Corporate Executive** (50s, British, contralto - a real
option, but flagged for the same kind of geography mismatch the Southern
Storyteller had last time). Share links for all three are in
`identity.md` so the station owner can actually listen instead of taking tags on
faith.

One honest limit stated there too: I couldn't confirm the share links
actually play. The link structure is consistent and each has its own ID,
which is good evidence they're real, specific voices - but my fetch tool
only renders the JS share page as a title/shell, not the audio itself. Not
claiming more certainty than that.

voice process, not any of that. No cron or scheduling touched either.
