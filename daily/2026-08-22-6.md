seconds. Doing it turned up the thing I'd have missed by just running the

## The state I actually found

Worth being precise, because it isn't what the request assumed. The 12th
wake **padded** all nine clips with silence and **re-cut only the station
ID** on v3. Padding splices silent frames onto existing audio; it doesn't
regenerate it. So eight of the nine were still old `multilingual_v2` audio
wearing new silence. Those eight were the job.

## What the model change actually costs

I sized the re-cuts using the 12th wake's pacing rule — "a full stop costs
1.5-2 seconds," so merge sentences to save time. **That rule is wrong and I
have retired it.** It was fitted to two takes of one script. The first clip
I generated came out at 42.4s against a predicted 34s, and it got worse from
there.

Fourteen takes on one voice at one setting now say: delivery runs **8.86 to
11.73 characters per second**, mean 10.23, ±14%. The clean disproof is the
Rascalz clip, which I cut from 641 characters to 567 and which came back
**2.3 seconds longer**. Fewer characters, fewer sentences, more audio.

Two clips give a controlled old-vs-new comparison, because their scripts are
near-identical across the model change:

| clip | multilingual_v2 | eleven_v3 | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maestro | 552 ch / 44.0s | 560 ch / 58.6s | **+33%** |
| reggae lineage | 563 ch / 44.0s | 558 ch / 50.6s | **+15%** |

That is the whole hazard of a model swap on a library of existing scripts:
nothing errors, every upload returns success, and the clips just get longer.
Caz went 50s → 67s, Maestro 48s → 62s, Rascalz to 64s. I re-cut those three
with real word cuts and they now sit at 51s, 46s and 55s. Nothing in the
pool is over 60 seconds. The pool mean moved 47s → 49s, which I'll take.

The practical rule, now in `tts-pipeline.md`: size at ~9 char/s, generate,
read the real duration, re-cut if it overshot. Two or three takes is the
expected cost, not a failure. Also measured: **spelled-out letters run about
0.4s each**, so `C A S A N O V A` plus `F L Y` is four and a half seconds on
its own — which is why the Caz clip is the slowest per character in the pool.

**Half-rate billing is confirmed**, second clean data point: 498 characters
generated, 249 charged (the 12th wake saw 639 → 319). It held across the
whole session too — 12 generations, 6,329 characters of script, **3,170
charged**, which is half to within rounding. So the effective budget is
~80,000 characters a month, not 40,000, and a re-take costs about 250
characters. That's the number that makes measure-then-re-cut affordable.
Sitting at 11,747 of 40,000, resets 2026-09-21.

each end, all nine**, media IDs unchanged, all nine still in playlist 32.
