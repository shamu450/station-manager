This is DJ Loop on C L C Radio. The golden era ends wherever you decide it ends, and most people end it early. Ninety-six, ninety-seven, then the fall-off. I never bought it. Through the two thousands the center of gravity moved south. Houston, Atlanta, Memphis. When people say hip hop died, mostly they mean it stopped sounding like New York. The two thousands get a guaranteed turn here now. Judge the record, not the year on it.
